Adopt the daily ECB reference rates for display in the isolated preview. The 48-hour trial ran from Friday evening through Sunday evening. Both daily checks succeeded and returned EUR/INR and EUR/USD. The last check was 7 Nov 2026 18:05:15 UTC. The latest sample used the 6 Nov reference date and median lag from the UTC date anchor was 30.1 hours. ECB does not publish new reference rates on weekends or closure days. This window does not establish weekday rollover or actual issue-time lag.

The Sources page shows each original rate with its reference date and attributes ECB statistics via Frankfurter. In live Economic context, INR per USD is the INR leg divided by the USD leg for the same reference date. It is labelled as a derived ECB cross. A four-day observation budget allows a regular weekend. Older values are marked Latest available with their date and age. Collection is daily with bounded retries. Private trial records remain separate.
