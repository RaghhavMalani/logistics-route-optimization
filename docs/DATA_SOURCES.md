anything actually reads it. Generated from the registry, so a source
cannot be described here and absent from the code.

A name in this table is **not** a claim of integration. `PLANNED` means
named with no adapter, and it is listed so the commercial path is
visible rather than implied.

## Licence notes

### Deterministic AIS replay

Generated by this project. Simulated positions, labelled as such on every surface.

Not observed traffic and never presented as such. It exists so the product is demonstrable without an AIS licence.

**Synthetic.** Modelled rather than observed, and labelled as such wherever drawn.

### AISStream

Free community AIS. Not licensed as a commercial production backbone.

Terms: <https://aisstream.io/>

Suitable for the student and demo builds. Deliberately barred from COMMERCIAL by its licence rather than by a code path.

### GDACS

Open data; commercial use and redistribution permitted with attribution.

Disaster alerts. No adapter wired yet.

### GDELT

Open data; commercial use and redistribution permitted with attribution.

The feed Global Eye ingests and merges into the event register.

### Global Fishing Watch

APIs are for non-commercial purposes. Excellent for research and government work; cannot back a commercial deployment.

Terms: <https://globalfishingwatch.org/our-apis/documentation/>

Identity across 40+ registries, AIS-gap and encounter events. The clearest example of why licence is a first-class field: it is one of the best sources available and is barred from COMMERCIAL and GOVERNMENT alike.

### IMF PortWatch

Open data; commercial use and redistribution permitted with attribution.

Port call and throughput statistics behind the port registry.

### Kpler

Commercial vessel tracking and port activity under contract.

Terms: <https://www.kpler.com/>

No adapter exists.

### NASA FIRMS

Open data; commercial use and redistribution permitted with attribution.

Active fire detections. No adapter exists.

### Natural Earth

Public domain.

Terms: <https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/>

Coastlines and borders the chart draws.

### Open-Meteo

Free for non-commercial use; commercial tiers available. Attribution required.

Terms: <https://open-meteo.com/en/terms>

The forecast surface the weather layer and the twin already read.

### Open-Meteo Marine

As Open-Meteo. Wave height, swell, SST and currents.

Terms: <https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/marine-weather-api>

Wave and swell fields the environment layer does not yet draw. No adapter is wired; the seam is the weather service.

### Spire Maritime

Commercial satellite and terrestrial AIS under contract. Redistribution terms are per-agreement.

Terms: <https://spire.com/maritime/>

No adapter exists. Named so the commercial path is visible.

### USGS earthquakes

Open data; commercial use and redistribution permitted with attribution.

Public seismic feed. No adapter wired yet.

## Terms are checked, not assumed

Licence terms above are recorded from each provider's published terms as
understood at the time of writing, and every entry carries its URL. They
are a starting point for a procurement conversation, not legal advice.
Global Fishing Watch in particular states its APIs are for non-commercial
purposes: it is one of the best sources available for this domain and is
barred from both `COMMERCIAL` and `GOVERNMENT`, which is exactly why
licence is a field the resolver reads rather than a note in a README.
