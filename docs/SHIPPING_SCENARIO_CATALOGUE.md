## Reproduction and interpretation

Enter **Company demo** normally, open **Global intelligence → Shipping demonstrations**, select an ID, read its basis, acknowledge and calculate. Every selection is an explicit fictional owned call. Export inputs/result from that disclosure. Retrying the same requestId references the durable ledger; a deliberate new selection starts another call. Source fixtures and canonical fixture originals stay visitor-private and never enter public forecasting/arrival cohorts. GETs do not select presets.

The catalogue covers decision types and refusals, not all emergencies or operational safety. Most presets share a 240 nm scalar passage, 12 kn baseline, 10 kn alternative, 8–18 kn envelope, hourly gust/visibility fixture and explicit modeled consumption. S11 uses 16 kn, S12 permits a 6 h departure hold, S15 truncates hourly coverage. Each returned `inputs` is the authoritative reproducible declaration. Comparison instants include common elapsed sailing; final numbers come from the engine.

Reroute S06–S08 uses existing `routes_for` geometry, branch constraints and strategic evaluation. It is **not connected to the port-approach advisory journey** in this milestone; no route is represented as approved navigation, and no map line alone proves safety. S16 supplies the connected Company → Port → Vessel → simulated outcome example.

S01 is a REAL_SOURCE_CONTEXT inspection, while its optional passage calculation uses explicitly labeled software weather. A current report does not make a fictional hull real.
