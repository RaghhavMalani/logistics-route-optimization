# Threats to validity

- The two corpora represent generators. The vessel generator reproduces a published benchmark design, and the cargo generator creates synthetic mixes and hidden outcomes. Neither estimates a prospective fleet effect. Unscoreable cases narrow the population represented by paired comparisons.
- Penalties, insurance terms, inventory rate, fuel bands and handling delays are scenario declarations. CIF + 10% is a modelling assumption. An unknown contribution or coverage basis can reverse an apparent advantage in a known subtotal. Customer priority affects a declared constraint, and undeclared margin has no invented value.
- Lognormal duration, the fizzle mixture, the Gaussian copula, exposure multipliers, the reopening signal and quantile interpolation are assumptions. Retained weather members, when available, still require an assumed wind-to-delay relationship. The thresholds and cost curves may perform differently under alternate dependence or tail models. The global indices condition on one finite inner design and the selected ranges.
- A single hidden future per test case preserves paired scoring while adding outcome variation. Bootstrap intervals describe sampling across the corpus under its exchangeability assumption. They exclude estimation error in declarations and source revisions. Rare losses and contingent capacity can dominate a real tail.
- The Ever Given case uses recorded milestones and a hypothetical hull, cargo mix and actions. S10 uses a frozen weather teaching fixture. These cases illustrate mechanisms and cannot establish actual losses, insurance settlements, or causal savings.
- IMF PortWatch publishes with lag and can revise observations. A retained observation within its usual publication window is still lagged. Forecast intervals and selected forecast horizons cannot replace a contemporaneous berth allocation, an observed wait, or a confirmed onward transfer.
- A prospective frozen comparison with retained as-of sources, declared shipment terms, independent verified arrivals and cargo losses could falsify the practical ranking and benefit claims. Persistent uncertainty miscalibration, weaker capture or higher paired p90 regret under the registered criteria would reject promotion. No policy or commercial execution changes follow from the illustrative results.
