## Exposure and useful review work

The existing visitor-owned fleet supplies the voyage declarations. The Company overview has a small sourced-review queue entry; its links open the existing Global Eye intelligence panel with the selected event and voyage. The panel supports event-to-voyage and voyage-to-event inspection, source originals, validity, map focus and calculation eligibility. Shared presentation additions are documented in [the additive teammate contract](team/INTELLIGENCE_CONTRACT_ADDITIONS.md); no teammate geometry or screen redesign is included.

Exposure has four explicit states:

- **CONFIRMED_DECLARED_EXPOSURE:** a declaration for the current operational call names the reviewed asset and route membership, and its passage time overlaps the published interval. This confirms the declaration's relationship to the notice, not an actual transit or closure.
- **POSSIBLE_EXPOSURE:** the operator names a possible asset, or suitable declared/reviewed route geometry passes within the documented 2 km point-proximity threshold. Passage, applicability and timing still require confirmation. This is not a route-clearance calculation.
- **NO_DEMONSTRATED_EXPOSURE:** membership is unproven or declared passage does not overlap the interval. Destination, broad lane labels and schematic ship positions alone never establish passage.
- **INSUFFICIENT_DATA:** asset mapping, period, declaration or current-call identity is missing or incompatible.

The operator can record a possible-passage lead without changing ETA, creating a delay forecast or replacing another asset's approved declaration. Multiple asset declarations remain in the same visitor fleet and call. Relevant changes at the consumed asset still block continuation; a genuinely new call clears current declarations/leads while preserving history.

Queue order is a transparent heuristic: confirmed declared exposure 40, possible lead 20, insufficient data 5, otherwise 0; passage within 24 hours adds 15 or within 72 hours adds 10. Cancelled, expired and superseded sources score zero. These values order review work; they are not calibrated probabilities or estimated delay.
