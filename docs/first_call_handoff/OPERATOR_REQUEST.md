Subject: One future JNPA port call — request for an operational data contact

To: JNPA Traffic Department (cmt@jnport.gov.in)

Hello,

I am preparing a PortWatch capstone research pilot to measure anchorage waiting for one future JNPA call. Could you introduce the terminal scheduler, shipping agent or port operator who can authorize and confirm this small data handoff?

The first pilot needs:

1. One upcoming call's canonical VIA/port-call reference, terminal, vessel name and verified nine-digit MMSI, supported by an agent or vessel identity record.
2. Before arrival, the scheduler's waiting-hours estimate for that individual call, with its document/reference, issue time and first availability time. An aggregate queue figure or expected cargo completion time cannot supply this estimate.
3. After the call, confirmed anchorage arrival and all-fast timestamps, berth and log references, plus when each timestamp became available to the operator. We also record our independent receipt time.

Before collection, we would agree the call identity, timestamp meanings, timezone and permitted research use. In particular, we need to confirm the anchorage-arrival event and whether the terminal's berthing record represents all-fast. If Marine confirmation is needed, please advise the appropriate contact.

Two small CSV templates and a source-confirmation form are attached. A document/log extract with an operator-confirmed transcription is also workable; no private camera access is needed. We will archive forecasts and observed AIS before arrival and reconcile the export against your records afterward. One completed call establishes the handoff, not prediction accuracy or operational savings.

Please let me know whether an authorized contact and one future call can be nominated, and which transfer channel they prefer. The pilot does not require changes to vessel navigation or terminal operations.

Thank you,
PortWatch capstone project
