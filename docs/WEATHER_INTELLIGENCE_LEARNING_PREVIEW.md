## Automatic weather verification

An independent backend worker reconciles at startup and every six hours, even when the original fabric freshness scheduler is disabled. Forecasts, raw provider responses, response hashes, coordinates, units, nominal target dates, sample windows and first successful outcome receipt are retained on the preview's persistent `/app/state` volume.

The fixed Open-Meteo archive endpoint is requested with `models=era5`, GMT and matching units. Six days is the conservative eligibility delay, following the provider's documented 5–7 day ERA5 update lag. Only 24 exact returned UTC hours, finite values and a nearby response grid cell are accepted. The original raw response must exist and match its archived SHA-256 before reconciliation can resolve that claim. Incomplete originals, unsupported variables, incorrect units, wrong locations, missing/corrupt raw originals and missing outcomes remain unresolved. Repeated forecast receipts do not increase independent target counts. Forecast backfill cannot serve as an outcome. The legacy daily weather cache remains feature history and no longer bypasses this verification path. Station/operator outcomes remain a distinct source cohort; this preview automatically connects reanalysis, with no station feed implied.

Wind and gust targets are maxima of 24 hourly samples, rather than measured continuous station extrema. The restored precipitation aggregation sums hourly labels 00–23. Because precipitation is a preceding-hour sum, its actual interval runs from the previous day's 23:00 through the labelled day's 23:00. The inspector exposes this interval and preserves the original nominal archive date; it does not rewrite originals or substitute a different midnight-to-midnight aggregate. Provider definitions: [historical weather API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api), [ERA5 availability](https://github.com/open-meteo/open-data/blob/main/README.md).

Candidates need eight independent targets per retained block, at least 32 overall, and a 2% MAE improvement over raw forecasts in both validation and subsequent evaluation. Forecast values stay raw in the ledger; corrections and their intervals are separately frozen at the subsequent forecast's origin.

Validated frozen daily corrections can feed a labelled **daily research weather watch**, with variable-specific, explicitly assumed thresholds. The inspector traces raw → consumed value, correction ID and resulting watch change. No daily correction enters the hourly gust/visibility approach gate.
