## Official evidence

The PDF SHA-256 is `d38aef79fc102488d5172049ed82a25428d79c972034d8193314a74500d60ce2`. The publisher's catalogue shows the notice on a page last updated on 2 November. No separate effective time, expiry, cancellation or superseding instruction was identified in the checked material. Listing establishes the publisher's current presentation of the notice. Any live application would require confirmation by the Marine Department.

- [Issuer's notice, page 1](https://www.paradipport.gov.in/uploads/2026/10/Unseaworthy-Vessels.pdf)
- [Issuer's circulars catalogue](https://paradipport.gov.in/circulars-notices/)

The notice requires agents to review survey reports, machinery status and structural condition and declare known defects to the Marine Department before entering Paradip port limits. If a vessel is found with undisclosed critical mechanical or structural defects, the authority reserves the right to deny entry, suspend pilotage or require it to leave. The authority's discretion and the agent's disclosure duty are separate. Disclosure alone supplies no entry or pilotage clearance.

The same source survey considered a November firing warning listed by the National Hydrographic Office. The linked in-force PDF retrieved from its catalogue was dated 7 July 2025. The firing schedule alone also supplied no verified prohibition or release condition. It was excluded from this pilot.

## Voyage and decision to carry through the demo

Use the fictional PW Meridian on an INPRT call. The comparison uses the visitor's current future entry request, and S16 supplies a fictional Paradip call for rehearsal. Declare a known critical steering defect and that the agent has yet to disclose it. Both facts are scenario inputs. They make the notice applicable because this call crosses Paradip port limits and the defect is known before entry. The notice does not establish the fictional vessel's defect, position or suitability to proceed.

## Advisory trace

1. Company reviews the scoped declaration and approves the conditional referral. Freeze the notice identity, original hash, retained revision, receipt/check times, voyage and call identity, planned entry time and both declared defect/disclosure inputs.
2. Port reviews the frozen referral and issues a demo hold-and-disclose advisory. Port review in the visitor world is distinct from an actual Marine Department decision. Any changed restriction, defect declaration or disclosure state requires a linked successor before further approval.
3. Vessel acknowledges and accepts the conditional plan. The retained simulated outcome records the accepted hold-and-refer action with actual entry, execution and schedule effect unobserved.
